Get to know Concreat Holdings Philippines, Solid Cement, and Apo Cement.

Solid Cement and Apo Cement

Solid Cement

Located in Antipolo, Rizal, Solid Cement is a prominent plant known for its quality products and commitment to industry standards.

Apo Cement

Situated in Naga City, Cebu, Apo Cement is among the largest in the country and upholds a legacy of innovation and excellence in cement production.
