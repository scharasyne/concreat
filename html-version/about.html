About Concreat Holdings

Building on a legacy of excellence and innovation in the Philippines.

Our Story

The name "Concreat" (pronounced "concrete") embodies the legacy and aspirations of the company's new owners. A combination of the Consunji name and the word "create," Concreat Holdings is committed to building enduring value that embodies the integrity, reliability and resilience central to the family's heritage.

Through our learning management platform, we extend this commitment to education and professional development, providing courses and programs that help individuals and organizations build lasting capabilities and achieve sustainable success.

Our Mission

To provide world-class education and training programs that empower individuals and organizations to achieve excellence and create lasting value in their fields.

Our Vision

To be the leading learning institution in the Philippines, recognized for developing professionals who build the infrastructure and capabilities that shape our nation's future.
